# Hypothesis quality

Use this to decide whether a candidate is testable and whether its wording may be causal. Do not sum the checks, weight them, or rank candidates with a score. Trade-offs stay written reasons. The exit is still pursue, reframe, defer, or reject.

## Keep the objects apart

- An observation is what was noticed, with its source and uncertainty. It does not explain itself.
- A research question fixes scope.
- A hypothesis is a candidate proposition. Leave its status as candidate.
- A mechanism is a proposed process. A plausible process without a discriminating prediction is a story.
- A prediction is an observable implication written before the target result is used.
- A causal estimand is a defined contrast. The estimator and the numerical estimate come later, outside this skill.
- Evidence bears on a claim. The claim is not itself evidence.

Calling a candidate validated, proven, or the winner is outside this skill.

## Clarity

The statement names the system, the conditions, the variables, the direction, and the timeframe. Put the mechanism in its own field. Replace vague comparatives with a direction and a comparison. Remove clauses that let the candidate survive every outcome.

If the text is still an observation or a question, send it back to framing and reframe.

## Testability

All four are required before pursue:

- an observable tied to a measurement
- a prediction written before the target result is used
- a feasible check that can bear on that prediction
- the assumptions that connect the result to the candidate

When the size of the difference is unknown, leave the number empty. Record the direction, the smallest difference that would change the decision, or a range with a reason. A fabricated precision is not a better hypothesis.

If no check can bear on the prediction, reject or reframe. If the check is feasible only after a named measurement or access exists, defer and name that fact.

## Vulnerability

State a result that is incompatible with the candidate while its assumptions hold. A candidate that explains every outcome after the fact is not testable: reframe or reject.

A flat result is uninformative when precision is low, the manipulation failed, the measurement cannot resolve the difference, missingness dominates, or an assumption failed. Write those possibilities down before treating a flat result as a falsifier. Failure to reject a no-difference model is not evidence of absence and is not equivalence. Equivalence needs its own margin, chosen before the result.

## Discriminability

Pursue requires at least one rival whose predicted pattern differs from the candidate by more than the expected measurement uncertainty. When every serious rival predicts the same pattern, the check cannot separate them: reframe the check.

An indeterminate outcome is part of the hypothesis record. It says what would be learned when the result matches neither pattern, matches both, or arrives with a failed assumption.

## Mechanism

When the claim type is mechanistic, name the entities, the activities, the order, and the regime. List intermediate steps that can be measured. Extra narrative detail does not raise the claim. If a bias, a bug, or a measurement fault produces the same appearance with fewer assumptions, that account stays in the rival set.

A mechanism that departs from an established constraint says where it departs. Silent contradiction of a constraint is a reason to reframe, not a reason to pursue.

## Boundaries

State where, when, and for whom the candidate should apply, which versions of the intervention or exposure matter, and which populations, platforms, or scales are out of scope. If the conclusion the proposal wants sits outside those boundaries, reframe.

## Assumptions

Keep five lists separate:

- scientific assumptions about the process
- measurement assumptions
- identification assumptions, when the claim is causal
- statistical or model assumptions
- implementation assumptions, including software, alignment, and baselines

Mark each as checkable, only partly diagnosable, or untestable with what is available. A pursue judgment that hangs on an untestable assumption stays explicitly conditional on it. If that assumption would flip the meaning of the check and cannot be probed, defer.

## Causal language

Causal verbs are allowed only when the estimand is written: population or system, intervention or exposure, comparator, outcome, horizon, population summary, and the identification assumptions. Those assumptions cover a well-defined intervention, exchangeability, overlap, interference, measurement, and missingness. For an observational contrast, also name the design analogue that would have produced the same contrast.

Without that estimand, the allowed wording is associated with, co-varied with, or predicted in the evaluated setting. Reframe any stronger verb.

These do not identify a causal effect on their own:

- a forecast or a model score
- temporal order by itself
- a covariate adjustment
- conditioning on a mediator, which changes the target from a total contrast to a direct one
- conditioning on a common effect of the exposure and the outcome, which can open a non-causal path

An intervention contrast does not establish the pathway. Pathway language waits for a prediction about an intermediate that a rival does not share.

## Operationalization

For each construct record the variable, its role, the instrument or method, the unit, the timing, the system, and the reason for any cut point. A convenient proxy is not the construct. Say what would show the instrument is measuring something else, whether missingness depends on the exposure or the outcome, and whether groups, times, sites, or devices are comparable.

No validity evidence in this regime means the claim narrows to what the instrument actually measures, or the judgment defers until a calibration exists. That calibration is a measurement check. It is not the scientific result.

## Exit

Do not invent a quality number. Return pursue, reframe, defer, or reject with the failed or satisfied checks named. Protocol lock, sample size, and the analysis plan belong to rk-method after a pursue judgment.
